## On the cards now

All from Old Book Illustrations, public domain, tailpieces and vignettes on white — Colly's own register.

- **About Kalyan** — an oil lamp with a crouching griffin: the scholar's lamp.
- **The Journal** — written sheets loosely gathered, a quill resting on them.
- **The Archive** — an open book, two closed ones, and a candle.
- **Receiving Your Emails** — a man in eyeglasses reading his mail with a surprised look.
- **Substack** — a laurel wreath with a pen and a chalk holder crossed over it.
- **Instagram** — the Meyers bellows camera, kept from the earlier round.
- **X** — Edison's electric pen with its wet-cell battery, 1870s: quick writing by machine.
- **RSS** — the 1683 post rider, kept.

Alternatives from the same source: a publisher's mark with beehive and lyre; a vignette of scrolls and books; a man reading behind a great leather book; an orator at his papers; a geared copper-plate press.
