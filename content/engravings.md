## On the cards now

- **About Kalyan** — Hyderabad: the tombs of the Qutb Shahi kings at Golconda, an 1870s encyclopaedia woodcut. Alternative below: the Char Minar, an 1850 engraving.
- **The Journal** — Anantapur: Gooty fort on its rock, from a 19th-century watercolour by Justinian Gantz. No engraving of Anantapur exists on Commons; this is the one period picture of the district. Alternative: St Andrew's Church, Bangalore, an 1867 *Illustrated London News* woodcut, if Bengaluru should take this card.
- **The Archive** — Kolkata: the Writers' Buildings with the Holwell obelisk, an 1812 aquatint. Alternative: Government House, 1824.
- **Receiving Your Emails** — Delhi: the Qutb Minar, 1858. Alternative: the Kutub Minar from an 1872 travel book.
- **Substack** — quill, inkwell, books and globe, c. 1900. Alternative: an 1875 inkstand.
- **Instagram** — a bellows camera from the Meyers encyclopaedia plate, 1890s. Alternative: the Loman camera from the same plate.
- **X** — a spark transmitter, 1906.
- **RSS** — a post rider blowing his horn, a 1683 woodcut.
