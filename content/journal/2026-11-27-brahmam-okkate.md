Annamacharya’s kirtana *Brahmam okkate*, “the Brahman is one”, sung a cappella by the American choir Chanticleer in an arrangement by its own tenor, Vineel Garisa Mahal. It is really nice to see the adaptation, and it spreads the universal message.

The message, verse by verse: there is no high or low; Sri Hari is the inner self of everyone. The king’s sleep and his servant’s sleep are one. The ground under the Brahmin and the ground where the outcaste lives are one. Day and night are the same for the richest and the poorest. The sun that falls on the elephant falls on the dog. And the name of Venkateswara protects the virtuous and the sinner alike.
