---
title: "Who decides what a Money Bill is"
date: 2026-08-14
tags: [GS2, Polity]
type: note
---
The Speaker's certificate under Article 110(3) is final as to whether a Bill is a Money Bill, and Article 122 keeps the courts out of proceedings in Parliament. Yet in *Rojer Mathew*, 2019, a five-judge bench doubted whether *Puttaswamy (Aadhaar)*, 2018, had applied the test correctly and referred the question to a larger bench. The honest one-line answer is therefore: final in the House, and under reference in the Court.
