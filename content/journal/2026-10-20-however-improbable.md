How often have I said to you that when you have eliminated the impossible whatever remains, *however improbable*, must be the truth?

<cite>Sherlock Holmes, in Arthur Conan Doyle’s [*The Sign of the Four*](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=The+Sign+of+the+Four+Arthur+Conan+Doyle), 1890, chapter 6</cite>
