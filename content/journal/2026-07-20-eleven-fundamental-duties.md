---
title: "Eleven, not ten"
date: 2026-07-20
tags: [Notes, Polity]
type: note
---
The Fundamental Duties were ten when the 42nd Amendment inserted Article 51A in 1976, on the Swaran Singh Committee's recommendation. The eleventh, a parent's duty to provide opportunities for education to a child between six and fourteen, came with the 86th Amendment in 2002, the same amendment that made Article 21A. Answers that say "ten" are dating themselves by twenty-four years.
