Ambedkar called Article 32 "the very soul of the Constitution and the very heart of it" in the Constituent Assembly on 9 December 1948. Examiners see the phrase misquoted as "heart and soul" in most answers. The order matters less than the article number: it is 32, the right to move the Supreme Court, not 226, which is the High Courts' writ power and lives in Part VI.
